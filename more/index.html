🗐 Pages

Slash pages are a set of commonly named pages often found on the indie web. Check out slash pages for the canonical source.

Here is my take with a few additional items that might catch on.

  • /about: who am I?
  • /accessibility: how this site is designed for all users regardless of ability
  • /ai: my AI usage, personally
  • /blogroll: curated collection of blogs I follow and recommend to others
  • /books: recommended readings that have influenced my thinking and work
  • /boycott: companies and products I avoid and my reasoning behind these decisions
  • /buttons: collection of web buttons and badges for a touch of internet nostalgia
  • /certifications: my professional certifications and achievements
  • /changelog: detailed history of updates and changes to this website
  • /colophon: what’s this website made of?
  • /contact: how to reach me professionally and personally
  • /contributions: open source projects and community work I’ve contributed to
  • /donations: causes and organizations I support financially
  • /guestbook: leave your mark and join others who have visited this site
  • /hello: friendly introduction and welcome to new visitors
  • /hire: information for those interested in my professional services
  • /interests: list of things I like in no particular order.
  • /links: useful resources, tools, and websites I recommend exploring
  • /media: videos, talks, and other media recommended by me
  • /next: upcoming projects, goals, and initiatives I’m planning
  • /nope: things I won’t do and my reasons why
  • /now: what I’m up to, now
  • /postroll: essays I like
  • /predictions: my predictions
  • /projects: current and past work showcasing my skills and interests
  • /quotes: words of wisdom that resonate with me from various sources
  • /save: discount codes for things I recommend
  • /services: professional services I offer and expertise available for hire
  • /subscribe: ways to follow my work and receive updates without commitment
  • /uses: tools, software, and gear that power my daily workflow
  • /verify: information to confirm my identity across various platforms
  • /where: where I am right now
