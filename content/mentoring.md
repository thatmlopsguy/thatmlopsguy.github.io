# 🧑‍🏫 Mentoring

You're seeking mentoring. Do you need personalized one-on-one guidance as an early or mid-career
professional? I offer mentoring sessions in various areas of cloud architecture, platform engineering,
and DevOps practices. Over the years, I have had the privilege of guiding many aspiring software engineers,
helping them achieve their career goals, improve their skills, and navigate the challenges of their roles.

These sessions are designed to help individuals enhance their skills and knowledge in these domains.
You can reach out to me to schedule a session or inquire about the topics covered.

In these mentoring sessions, we will cover a wide range of topics related to cloud architecture,
platform engineering, and DevOps practices. The sessions are designed to be interactive and hands-on,
ensuring that participants gain practical experience and a deep understanding of the concepts discussed.

## Topics Covered

- Cloud Architecture
- Platform Engineering
- Observability and Monitoring
- Security and Compliance
- Kubernetes and Containerization
- Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)
- GitOps Best Practices
- Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
- Cloud Native Development
- Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)
- Cloud Cost Optimization
- Machine Learning and AI in Cloud Environments
- Agentic Development Platforms

## What can you expect from this program?

My mentorship methodology is meticulously designed to cover a broad spectrum of developmental facets,
ensuring a holistic approach to your growth and success.

- **Customized Mentorship Programs**: Tailored to fit your unique aspirations and requirements,
    these programs can enhance your coding abilities, introduce you to emerging tech, or equip you
    for job interviews. Each plan is crafted to match your individual needs.

- **In-depth Code Analysis**: Benefit from comprehensive critiques of your code, fostering adherence
    to top-tier standards, enhancing code integrity, and facilitating the development of resilient
    applications.

- **Professional Development Support**: Aligning with your professional ambitions, we refine your CV,
    hone interview techniques, and strategize for overcoming workplace hurdles.

- **Interactive Knowledge Sharing**: Engage in frequent dialogues on software engineering subjects,
    keeping abreast of sectoral evolutions and enriching your expertise within the discipline.

My goal is to build a bond, where we have a weekly meeting of about an hour, over the course of multiple
weeks or even months. I’m also available for one-off meetings where I answer one specific question one time.

## What do I expect from you?

You’re going to need a good enough internet connection to talk and share your screen. You need to be able
to speak English or Portuguese. You’re going to need at least a basic understanding of computer science,
I can’t teach you that from scratch.

Finally, as my preferred “office hours” are between Saturday 10:00 and 12:00 Central European Time (Paris),
our schedules have to match as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long are the mentoring sessions?

Each mentoring session typically lasts about an hour. We can adjust the duration based on your needs and the
complexity of the topics discussed.

### How often will we meet?

We usually have weekly meetings, but the frequency can be tailored to your schedule and learning pace.

### What if I have a specific question outside of our regular meetings?

I am available for one-off meetings to address specific questions or challenges you may encounter. You can drop me a
message via [contact](contact.md) with the details of your specific question or challenge.

### What are the prerequisites for this mentorship program?

You should have a basic understanding of computer science concepts and be able to communicate in English or Portuguese.
A reliable internet connection is also required. A quiet and distraction-free environment is recommended for effective
mentoring sessions. A computer with a functioning microphone is also necessary to facilitate effective
communication during the sessions. The computer should also have the necessary software installed ([signal](https://signal.org))
for screen sharing and communication. It should have enough processing power and memory to run the necessary software
smoothly, such as having a multi-core processor and at least 8GB of RAM.

### Can I change the focus of my mentorship program?

Yes, the mentorship program is customized to your needs, and we can adjust the focus as your goals and interests evolve.

### How much does the mentorship program cost?

For now, the mentorship program is free of charge. Updates regarding any potential costs will be communicated in advance.

### What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?

If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, please notify me in advance so we can find a suitable alternative time.
Cancellations or rescheduling should ideally be communicated at least 24 hours before the scheduled session.

### How do I prepare for a mentoring session?

To make the most out of each session, come prepared with specific questions or topics you want to discuss.
Reviewing previous session notes and doing any recommended pre-work will also help maximize the value of our time together.

### What if I have feedback or suggestions for the mentorship program?

I welcome any feedback or suggestions you may have to improve the mentorship experience. Your input helps tailor the
program to better meet your needs and enhance its overall effectiveness. You can provide feedback or suggestions by
reaching out to me directly via [contact](contact.md). I appreciate your input and will consider it to improve the
mentorship experience.
